# Welcome to the ounce-hb Wiki

`ounce-hb` is an independent, clean-room runner for **Nintendo Switch homebrew**: unencrypted
NRO executables and NSO modules built from source with [devkitPro](https://devkitpro.org/) and
[libnx](https://github.com/switchbrew/libnx).

Built on top of [ounce-core](https://github.com/hffgzktbcc-blip/ounce-core), ounce-hb aims for
verifiable correctness, diagnostic clarity, and complete legal transparency.
